Geographical Map for Food Aid Provision

Please click the icon to see a map showing the geographical location of food providers across Leeds, including foodbanks, Food Pantries, drop-ins, Community Hubs, cafes, businesses, and enterprises.

Instructions for how to use the map are on the front page as you access the map. If you have any questions or comments about using the map, please use the contact page.

You can search the map for projects near you, by keyword or project name, by using the map tools or the filter options provided. The map shows details of services and projects, that may include their…
• Location and address
• Contact details
• A link to their website
• The provision they offer
• Opening times
• Eligibility and referral routes

This map is a work in progress, so if you run a food provision project, temporary or permanent, we would love to have your details on the map. You can add information about your service / project by filling in the form here. ** If your project is temporary, please provide an end date on the form and your project will automatically be removed from the map on that date.

If you have any updates or want to make changes to the information regarding your provision, you can update these using the link provided when you first registered on the map. If you are unable to locate the email, please use the contact page and the team will assist you.
