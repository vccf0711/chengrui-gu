Donate Food and Items

Click the heading above for information regarding donating Food and Items to support food aid providers / organisations who are supporting those on the margins. This link will take you through to the Street Support Leeds Website. Just scroll down and you will see where you can type in the details of what you would like to donate. The above heading will take you through to the Street Support Leeds Website. Scroll down and you will see different organisations and what they currently need.
